Our story

Started because the old way wasn't working.

Pathway was founded in 2022 by a small group of people who'd spent years on both sides of hiring — applying, getting ghosted, and then later hiring teams of their own and watching great candidates fall through the cracks.

We wanted to build something simpler. A place where job seekers feel respected, where employers can actually find the people they need, and where everyone gets a clear answer instead of an endless wait.

Today we're a team of 38, headquartered in Brooklyn with people working from 12 different cities. We're growing fast — but slowly enough to do this right.

What we believe

The principles we hire and build by

People first

Behind every application is a person hoping for an answer. We design as if our families were on the other end.

Bias toward clarity

Vague pricing, hidden filters, "we'll be in touch" — the industry runs on ambiguity. We pick the opposite.

Move with care

We ship quickly when we're certain and slowly when we're not. Speed at the cost of harm isn't speed.
