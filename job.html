About the role

Aria Health is reshaping how patients interact with their care teams. We're hiring a Senior Product Designer to lead the design of our patient-facing mobile experience — a product used by hundreds of thousands of people every week to schedule visits, message their doctors, and track their treatment plans.

You'll partner closely with product, engineering, and clinical research to ship designs that are accessible, evidence-based, and genuinely care about the people on the other side of the screen.

What you'll do

  • Own the end-to-end design of our patient mobile experience — from research through launch.
  • Lead design discovery and run usability sessions with patients of varied ages and abilities.
  • Contribute to and evolve our design system, ensuring consistency across web and mobile.
  • Partner with engineers to ship pixel-precise work and stay involved through release.
  • Mentor mid-level designers and help raise the craft bar across the team.

What we're looking for

  • 5+ years of product design experience, with at least 2 years on consumer mobile.
  • A portfolio that shows clear thinking, visual craft, and impact — not just polished screens.
  • Comfort running your own research and translating findings into design decisions.
  • Experience designing for accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA) and across a wide range of devices.
  • Bonus: prior experience designing in healthcare, fintech, or other regulated industries.

Benefits

  • Fully remote across the US, with quarterly team gatherings.
  • Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance.
  • $2,500 yearly home-office stipend and a $1,500 learning budget.
  • Unlimited PTO with a 3-week minimum.
  • Meaningful equity in a growing company.
