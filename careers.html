How we work

Less process. More trust.

We're not a "move fast and break things" team. We're a "move thoughtfully and build something durable" team. Most decisions are made by the people closest to the work — managers exist to unblock, not to approve.

We write a lot, meet less, and ship every week. If that sounds like how you want to work, we'd love to talk.
