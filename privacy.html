Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Pathway collects, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have about it. We try to keep this readable — if anything is unclear, write to us.

Overview

Pathway, Inc. ("Pathway", "we", "us") operates the website at pathway.com and the related services that help people find jobs and help companies hire people. When you use our services, you trust us with information about you. We take that seriously.

The short version: we collect what we need to make the product work, we don't sell your personal information to third parties for advertising, and you can ask to see, export, or delete your data at any time.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details — your name, email address, and password when you sign up.
  • Profile content — work history, education, skills, resume files, and anything else you choose to put on your profile.
  • Applications and messages — content of applications you send to employers and any messages exchanged on the platform.
  • Payment information — if you're an employer paying for a plan, we collect billing details through our payment processor (we don't store full card numbers).

Information collected automatically

  • Device and log data — IP address, browser type, operating system, pages viewed, and timestamps.
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address.
  • Usage signals — which features you use and how often, so we can improve them.

Information from third parties

  • If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email from that provider.
  • If an employer imports your profile from elsewhere, we receive whatever data they provided.

How we use it

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate the service — show jobs, route applications, send messages between you and employers.
  • Match candidates and roles — the more complete your profile, the better the matches.
  • Communicate with you — confirm account actions, send job alerts you've opted into, respond to support requests.
  • Improve the product — understand which features help people get hired and which don't.
  • Protect the platform — detect fraud, abuse, and security threats.
  • Comply with the law — when we're legally required to.

When we share information

We share information only in these specific cases:

  • With employers — when you apply to a role, the employer receives your application and profile.
  • With service providers — companies that help us run Pathway (hosting, email delivery, payment processing). They're bound by contracts that limit what they can do with your data.
  • For legal reasons — when required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety.
  • In a business transfer — if Pathway is acquired or merged, your data may transfer to the new entity, subject to this policy.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how the product is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Some features won't work without them.

We use a small number of analytics tools to measure aggregate usage. We don't run third-party advertising trackers.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that's inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your account and the personal information associated with it.
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Object to certain processing or withdraw consent.

You can exercise most of these directly in your account settings. For anything else, email privacy@pathway.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days, except where we're required to keep it for legal or accounting reasons. Anonymous aggregate data may be retained indefinitely.

Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data: encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, regular security reviews. No system is perfectly secure, so we also recommend you use a strong, unique password and enable two-factor authentication.

Children's privacy

Pathway is not intended for use by anyone under 16. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our product evolves. If we make material changes, we'll notify you by email or through a notice in the product before the changes take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
